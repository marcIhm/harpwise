   - Support more chord progressions, e.g.:
     - I – V – vi – IV (The "Axis" or Pop Progression): C – G – Am – F
     - I – IV – V – I (Classic Rock, Folk, and Blues): C – F – G – C
     - ii – V – I (The Jazz Foundation): Dm7 – G7 – Cmaj7
     - I – vi – IV – V (1950s Doo-wop Progression): C – Am – F – G

   - Support a more complete set of chords, e.g.:
     - I – Major (e.g., C)
     - ii – minor (e.g., Dm)
     - iii – minor (e.g., Em)
     - IV – Major (e.g., F)
     - V – Major (e.g., G)
     - vi – minor (e.g., Am)
     - vii° – diminished (e.g., Bdim)


   - Funktionen
     - Tonic: I oder vi
     - Dominant: V oder vii°
     - Sub-Dominant: IV oder ii

   - Grease-Progression: I vi IV V I

   - Jazz: VI ii° V i
